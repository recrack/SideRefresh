## Selected prompt

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: Product Hunt gallery background
> illustration. Create an entirely abstract visual metaphor for continuity,
> local refresh, and a completed renewal cycle on a deep midnight navy matte
> background. Use one continuous luminous ribbon forming a calm open loop
> through original rounded geometric checkpoints, with a small mint completion
> pulse. Refined abstract 3D product illustration, crisp geometry, subtle glass
> and anodized-metal materials. Concentrate the loop on the right half and leave
> clean negative space on the left. Use dark navy, electric cyan, cobalt blue,
> and a small mint accent. Fully abstract: no phones, computers, devices,
> screens, UI, app icons, text, logos, trademarks, operating-system imagery,
> cables, clouds, arrows, people, or watermark. Do not imitate commercial
> products or imply wireless range or remote installation.

The first generated draft depicted hardware-like objects and was rejected after
the Apple trademark review. It is intentionally not checked into this project.
The selected abstract result was visually inspected before compositing.
