# 60-second demo storyboard

| Time | Picture | On-screen message | Evidence gate |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0–6s | SideRefresh icon, tagline, clean title frame. | Your agent builds it. SideRefresh keeps it running. | Use the canonical icon and exact tagline. |
| 6–14s | Simple four-step Build → Install → Use → Refresh cycle. | Free Personal Team development signing expires. | Do not state a universal number of days. |
| 14–24s | Open the public SideRefresh build and choose the checked-in SideRefresh Sample project. | Choose the checked-in SideRefresh Sample Xcode project. | Show one container only; hide paths, account, team, and signing data. |
| 24–32s | Choose the prepared iPhone already paired with Xcode. | Choose one iPhone already paired with Xcode. | Hide UDID, serial, IP, Tailnet, and unrelated devices. |
| 32–43s | Save, return to My app, and start Refresh now. Condense real waiting time. | SideRefresh rebuilds, signs, validates, and reinstalls through Xcode. | Overlay `Build wait condensed`; never animate a fake result. |
| 43–53s | Show the completed state, Last verified evidence, signing expiration, and next eligibility. | Verify the installation evidence and next refresh. | The installed app and evidence must match the recording. |
| 53–60s | Show repository, Apache 2.0, local boundary, languages, and final CTA. | Open source. Local by design. Mac and Xcode required. | Use public URLs and the legal notice. |

## Shot list

1. 3s title card, then 3s subtle abstract loop motion.
2. 8s expiration-cycle graphic.
3. 2s app launch; 8s SideRefresh Sample selection and confirmation.
4. 8s paired-iPhone selection and confirmation.
5. 3s save/My app; 2s Refresh now; 6s condensed real progress.
6. 6s verified result; 4s expiration and next-refresh evidence.
7. 4s GitHub/release card; 3s closing tagline and legal line.

Use clean cuts and one short dissolve at the closing card. Keep the cursor slow,
do not zoom into hidden identifiers, and avoid decorative device mockups or
Xcode/Apple artwork.
