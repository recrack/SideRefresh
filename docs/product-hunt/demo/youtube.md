# YouTube metadata

## Title

> SideRefresh — Keep agent-built iOS apps running on your iPhone

## Description

> SideRefresh is an open-source Mac app that refreshes one personal iOS app you own through Xcode Personal Team before development signing expires.
>
> In this 60-second walkthrough: choose one Xcode project, choose one already-paired iPhone, run a real refresh, and inspect the verified installation evidence and next refresh.
>
> Requirements: macOS 13 or later, Xcode, an Apple Account Personal Team, a physical iPhone, initial pairing and trust, Developer Mode, and one successful Xcode installation. SideRefresh does not install third-party IPAs, bypass Apple signing, make profiles permanent, or work without Xcode.
>
> Source and download: https://github.com/recrack/SideRefresh
> Manual: https://github.com/recrack/SideRefresh/blob/master/docs/MANUAL.md
>

## Upload settings

- Visibility: Public or Unlisted, never Private.
- Thumbnail: `docs/product-hunt/assets/demo/youtube-thumbnail-1280x720.png`
- Captions: upload `captions-en.srt`; optionally add `captions-ko.srt`.
- Product Hunt field: paste the full `https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=...` URL,
  not a short `youtu.be` URL.
- Audience and category: owner chooses truthfully in the live YouTube UI.
